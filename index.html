About Hourly Lucky Spin

🕒 24 Hourly Live Rounds

The wheel automatically spins at the top of every hour (12:00 AM to 11:00 PM). The live countdown indicates time remaining until the next hourly winner is drawn.

🎰 10 Lucky Number Slots

Every round features 10 selected numbers (from 1 to 100). The winning number is announced immediately after the wheel comes to a complete rest.

📜 Live History

The Last 3 Results section displays the latest winning numbers along with their exact 12-hour round times.
